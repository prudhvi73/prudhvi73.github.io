Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Our Discord bot collects the following data to function:

  • Discord User ID — to identify users across sessions
  • Server (Guild) ID — to manage per-server settings
  • Command usage data — command names, timestamps, success/failure (no message content)
  • Configuration preferences — settings you choose for the bot in your server

We do not collect: message content, DMs, voice data, email addresses, IP addresses, or any personally identifiable information beyond your Discord User ID.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and maintain bot functionality
  • Remember your server's configuration
  • Debug and fix issues (command errors only)
  • Prevent abuse and spam

3. Data Storage & Retention

Data is stored in a secure database. Configuration data persists until you remove the bot or request deletion. Command logs are retained for 30 days for debugging, then automatically purged.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Data may be disclosed only if required by law.

5. Your Rights

  • Request data deletion by contacting us or using the bot's /data delete command
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Opt out of analytics (where applicable)

6. Children's Privacy

Our bot is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Continued use of the bot constitutes acceptance.
